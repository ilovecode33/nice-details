Make the app installable and add an Install card to Settings. The bottom dock is always minimized and a floating sync status chip sits to its left, opening the sync sheet; the status bar and browser chrome follow the resolved theme. A web manifest, maskable icons, an apple-touch-icon, and a network-first service worker make the app a standalone PWA; Chromium gets an "Add to Home Screen" button, iOS and other browsers get share/menu instructions, and an installed window shows "Installed".
